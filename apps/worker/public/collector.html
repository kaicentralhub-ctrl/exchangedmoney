exchangedmoney

Our data collector

If you've seen exchangedmoney-collector in your logs, that's us.

What it reads

Public pages that publish exchange rates or currency conversion charges, such as central-bank reference rates and the card markup tables banks publish under the Cross-Border Payments Regulation.

How often

Once a day per page, one request at a time. We don't log in, submit forms or read anything that isn't public.

What we do with it

We keep a dated copy so we can show how rates and charges change over time, and we always say where a number came from.

Contact

If you'd like us to slow down or stop, email collector@exchangedmoney.com and we'll respond promptly.
